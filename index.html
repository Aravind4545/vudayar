The Master Behind The Art

A Living Tradition of Sacred Sculpting

Born on the auspicious day of Sri Ramanavami into a family of revered sculptors spanning three generations, Rajkumar Vudayar grew up with stone as his cradle and the chisel as his calling.

Guided by his father and guru, World-renowned Sculptor Srinatharatna Silpi, Rajkumar sculpted his first statue—Gautama Buddha—at a tender age. Choosing wholehearted devotion to sacred craftsmanship over conventional academics, he has since brought over 2,000 monumental statues to life across India and the globe.

Artisan Workflow

The Four Stages of Creation

How raw earth and timeless stone are transformed into living embodiments of culture through patient mastery.

01

Dhyana & Conceptual Design

Meditative conceptualization adhering to iconography rules, sacred proportions, and historical references before a single chisel touches stone.

02

Shila & Medium Selection

Careful selection of flawless monolithic granite, Krishna shila, Italian marble, or bell-metal bronze alloys suited for enduring centuries.

03

Hand Chiseling & Relief

Master sculptors carve rough blocks into anatomic grace, chiseling expressive facial features, ornate jewels, and sacred drapery.

04

Polishing & Final Consecration

Gentle finishing, hand buffing, and texturing to bring out the natural luster, depth, and divine presence of the completed sculpture.
